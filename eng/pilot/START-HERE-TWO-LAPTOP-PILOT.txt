BALLS TWO-LAPTOP PILOT

Use this same single ZIP on both Windows laptops.

1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the newly extracted folder.
3. Double-click CHECK PACKAGE.cmd.
4. Send the PASS or BLOCKED message back to Codex.

Do not run CHECK PACKAGE.cmd from inside the ZIP preview.
Do not launch Balls or change firewall settings until the package check passes.

The download and extraction locations do not matter. The checker finds the
bundled Balls files relative to itself and records that exact location for the
later host steps.
